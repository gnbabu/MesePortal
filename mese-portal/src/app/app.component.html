For Nursing Facilities: Important Ratesetting Message

The release of the Minimum Data Set (MDS) information was delayed due to the Ohio Department of Medicaid (ODM) awaiting data use approval from CMS. Updated reports were made available January 31 for review and correction. A second report will be released by February 7, for further review and correction. The last date for corrections is Friday, February 21. The final report will be posted the following week, on Friday, February 28.

Important reminder: Update 1099 address information in the Provider Network Management (PNM) module.

It is important that providers update all 1099 address information, including extra details like suite number or office number, to reduce the chance of non-delivery by postal service.
